# Visibility Controls

Visibility decides where Plex surfaces the collection. Agregarr models Plex’s three toggleable options.

## Primary Toggles

- **Users Home** – shows the collection on every non-owner home screen.
- **Server Owner Home** – shows the collection on the owner’s home screen.
- **Library Recommended** – shows the collection inside the library’s recommended tab.
- Leaving all three unchecked keeps the collection available in the library view only.

## Source-Specific Behaviour

- Overseerr **Individual Users** collections are forced to *Library tab* only because Plex ignores label restrictions on Home/Recommended.
- Overseerr **Server Owner** collections can only toggle *Server Owner Home*
- Default Plex hubs and Pre-Existing Collections inherit their existing visibility; any customisations apply the next time Agregarr syncs.

## Inactive Visibility

When you configure time restrictions and choose not to remove the collection outright, an additional visibility section appears for the inactive state. Use it to hide the collection from home screens while keeping it discoverable in the library.
